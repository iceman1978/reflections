How quotes are checked

Claude may only quote the works on this page. Before a reflection is shown, the app looks for every quotation in its own copy of that exact edition. If the words are there, you see the source's exact wording and a link to the passage. If they aren't, the reflection puts the idea in its own words and names where it came from, rather than risk a misquotation. Claude never writes the links: they always come from this list.
