Where the wisdom comes from

How quotes are checked

Reflections may only quote the works on this page. Before a reflection is shown, the app looks for every quotation in its own copy of that exact edition. If the words are there, you see the source's exact wording and a link to the passage. If they aren't, the reflection puts the idea in its own words and names where it came from, rather than risk a misquotation. The links never come from the AI: they always come from this list.
