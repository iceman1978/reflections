How your writing is kept private

Who can read your entries

Only you. Every entry is encrypted with a key that is locked by your passphrase. The server stores only the encrypted version, so whoever runs this site (or the hosting company) sees unreadable data. {% if hosted %}Signing in with Google only proves who you are; Google never sees your journal.{% endif %}

Because of this, nobody can reset your passphrase. If you forget it, your recovery key is the only way back in. Without either, the entries can't be recovered by anyone.

When something leaves the journal

  • Reflections and writing prompts: the text of the entry (or, for prompts, a few recent entries) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to write the reflection. Anthropic doesn't use API data to train its models by default.
  • Export (CSV): downloads an unencrypted copy to your device, only when you ask for it.

No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts are used. The only cookies keep you signed in and remember that this browser has unlocked your journal.
